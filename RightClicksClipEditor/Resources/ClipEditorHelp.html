🎬 RightClicks Clip Editor - User Guide

📋 Quick Start

Step 1: Play your video and find the start of the clip you want to extract
Step 2: Press I to set the IN point (start)
Step 3: Continue playing or seek to the end of your clip
Step 4: Press O to set the OUT point (end)
Step 5: Press Ctrl+A to add the clip to your list
Step 6: Repeat steps 1-5 for additional clips
Step 7: Press Ctrl+S to export all clips

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Spacebar Play / Pause video
I Set IN point at current position
O Set OUT point at current position
Left Arrow Step backward 1 frame
Right Arrow Step forward 1 frame
Shift+Left Step backward 1 second
Shift+Right Step forward 1 second
Ctrl+A Add current selection to clip list
Ctrl+S Save all clips to files
Ctrl+W Close editor window
F1 Show this help

🎯 Timeline Controls

The timeline provides visual feedback and precise control over your clips:

Playhead (Blue)

  • Shows: Current playback position
  • Click: Seek to any position in the video
  • Drag: Scrub through the video

IN Marker (Green)

  • Shows: Start point of your clip selection
  • Drag: Adjust the start point precisely
  • Set: Press I or click "Set IN" button

OUT Marker (Red)

  • Shows: End point of your clip selection
  • Drag: Adjust the end point precisely
  • Set: Press O or click "Set OUT" button

🔍 Zoom & Navigation

  • Zoom In: Ctrl+Mouse Wheel Up or click + button
  • Zoom Out: Ctrl+Mouse Wheel Down or click - button
  • Fit All: Click Fit button to see entire duration
  • Scroll: Shift+Mouse Wheel to scroll horizontally when zoomed
💡 Tip: Zoom in for frame-accurate editing! At high zoom levels, you can see individual frames and make precise cuts.

📁 Output Files

Clips are automatically saved with sequential numbering:

  • Location: Same folder as your source video
  • Naming: originalname_clip_001.mp4, originalname_clip_002.mp4, etc.
  • Format: Same format as source (MP4, AVI, MKV, etc.)
  • Quality: Re-encoded for frame accuracy (configurable in Settings)
💡 Tip: For faster exports with less quality loss, enable "Stream Copy" mode in Settings. This is keyframe-accurate instead of frame-accurate.

🎬 Clip List Management

  • Add Clip: Set IN/OUT points, then press Ctrl+A
  • Enable/Disable: Check/uncheck clips to control which ones are exported
  • Remove Clip: Click the "Remove" button next to any clip
  • Export All: Press Ctrl+S to save all enabled clips
⚠️ Note: Only clips with checkmarks will be exported when you click "Save All Clips".

🎵 Audio Clips

The Audio Clip Editor works similarly to the Video Clip Editor with these differences:

  • Waveform Display: Visual representation of audio amplitude
  • Sample-Accurate: Precise to the audio sample level
  • Loop Selection: Press L to loop the selected region
  • Fine Stepping: Shift+Left/Right steps by 10ms for precise editing

❓ Troubleshooting

Video won't load

  • Ensure FFmpeg is installed in %LOCALAPPDATA%\RightClicks\bin\
  • Check that the video file is not corrupted
  • Try a different video format

Clips export with wrong duration

  • Use re-encoding mode (not stream copy) for frame-accurate clips
  • Check that IN point is before OUT point
  • Verify the source video has accurate timestamps

Timeline markers not visible

  • Zoom out using Ctrl+Mouse Wheel Down or click "Fit" button
  • Markers may be outside the visible area - scroll with Shift+Mouse Wheel

⚙️ Tips & Best Practices

  • ✅ Use frame stepping (Left/Right) for precise IN/OUT points
  • ✅ Zoom in on the timeline for frame-accurate editing
  • ✅ Disable clips you don't want to export instead of removing them
  • ✅ Use descriptive file names by renaming after export
  • ✅ Test with a short clip first to verify quality settings
  • ⚠️ Large videos may take time to analyze - be patient on first load
  • ⚠️ Re-encoding takes longer but provides frame-accurate cuts
